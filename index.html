WHAT THE ROLE INVOLVES

Roles & Responsibilities

A System Administrator helps an organization keep its technology working safely and efficiently.

▣
01

Operating System Management

Install, configure, and update operating systems so computers and servers work properly.

♙
02

User Account Management

Create accounts, reset passwords, and give users only the permissions they need.

⌁
03

System Monitoring

Check system health, storage, memory, and network activity to find problems early.

↻
04

Backup & Recovery

Make backup copies of important data and restore them when data is lost or damaged.

⚙
05

Troubleshooting

Identify and fix hardware, software, and network problems reported by users.

⬡
06

Security & Updates

Apply security updates and follow safe practices to reduce risks and unauthorized access.
